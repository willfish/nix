Package Proof and Market Yourself

You do not market the transition by saying you are an AI Systems Engineer. You market it by showing production decisions that a backend engineer, platform engineer, or staff engineer would respect.

The Win

You will turn the lesson artifacts into a public-facing case study outline and a concise positioning statement.

The Proof Stack

Architecture

Workflow graph, tool contracts, state model, MCP decision, approval points, and fallback paths.

Reliability

Eval matrix, regression checks, guardrails, failure modes, and examples that demonstrate restraint.

Operations

Trace examples, metrics, cost and latency budgets, error handling, and what you would alert on.

Positioning

A clear sentence connecting your backend background to production AI systems ownership.

Exercise: Draft the Case Study

Write this as if it will become the README or blog post for your first portfolio project.

A strong case study shows constraints, decisions, evidence, and tradeoffs.

Positioning Template

Backend engineer specializing in production AI systems: agent workflows, tool orchestration, evals, observability, guardrails, human review, and reliable integration with existing software systems.

Use that as the working headline until your first case study gives you sharper language.
